gemini.analysis.nodal

gemini.analysis.nodal(plant, well, conditions, on_error='raise', units=None)[source]

Return the IPR and VLP curves and the operating point of a production or injection well.

The IPR (inflow performance) is the sandface pressure the reservoir delivers at each rate. The VLP (vertical lift performance) is the wellhead pressure the well, the ESP and the riser deliver from that inflow at each rate: the node is the wellhead, and the operating point is where the VLP falls to the wellhead pressure the surface plant holds. The solve is the one of solve_production_rate. The plant is not changed: the ESP frequency of a case is set on a copy.

An injection well has the same shape. ipr is the sandface pressure the aquifer needs at each rate and vlp the wellhead pressure that delivers it down the well, `` solve_injection_pressure``; the operating point is the pressure a case’s rate needs, or the rate a case’s pressure pushes in. A rate the column of brine injects alone needs no pump: its point on vlp is None, and in operating_point a value None with a reason.

Parameters:

  • plant (Module) – A plant with parameter values.

  • well (str) – Dotted path of the module that holds the whole chain, for example "doublet": one ProductionReservoir and either one full ProductionWell or an ESP with the below_esp and above_esp segments. For injection, the path of an InjectionWell or of its InjectionReservoir, "doublet.injector", or of what holds one of each and no production chain.

  • conditions (dict or list of dict) – One case, or several for a what-if. Each has wellhead_pressure [Pa], rates, the [low, high] bracket [kg/s] of the curves and of the solve (a trial outside the well model’s domain fails the case), and frequency [Hz] when the chain has an ESP. Numbers are SI, or a Quantity. An injection case has rates, temperature [K], the temperature at the wellhead, and rate or wellhead_pressure, which says what to solve for.

  • on_error ({"raise", "record"}, optional) – A case that fails raises; "record" puts its problem in error and runs the others.

  • units (dict, optional) – A map from quantity to unit for the returned values, such as {"pressure": "bar"}.

Returns:

Plain JSON {well, cases}. Each case holds conditions as given, ipr and vlp as {rate, pressure}, each a value with 21 points from the low to the high rate, operating_point as {rate, wellhead_pressure, sandface_pressure}, each a value, and error. Under on_error="record" a case whose bracket leaves the well model’s domain or has no sign change has None curves and operating point and its problem in error.

Return type:

dict
