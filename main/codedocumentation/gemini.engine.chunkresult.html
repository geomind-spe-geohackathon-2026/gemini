gemini.engine.chunkresult

ChunkResult: what an executor gives for one module over the rows of a chunk.

result is the ModuleResult tree mapped to arrays of rows (children included): a literal broadcasts, a list of values is an array of shape (rows, n), None is NaN (ModuleResult.row reads it back as None for a declared X | None) and an output has the dtype its declaration gives on the plain path. failed marks the failed rows and error(row) is the exception of that row. states are the states after a stepped call, stepped tells that the call was stepped and breaks lists (path, reason) of the places an executor ran a module for real instead of in its own way.
