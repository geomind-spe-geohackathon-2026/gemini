gemini.study.minimize

gemini.study.minimize(plant, case, objective, evaluations, seed, *, plan=None, on_progress=None)[source]

Return the design of a plant that minimises a metric, searched in the space the plant states.

The variables are the plant’s own: every leaf with a range (continuous, or integer when the parameter is) and every leaf with options (a choice among strings), and the number of doublets when size.doublets has a range. Leaves with the same parameter name, the same role and an equal range are one variable (three chillers of one range are one capacity); the order is that of the file. gm.check runs first and raises its issues, so a plant is sized first (gm.study.size completes a template plant).

Each trial copies the plant, sets the variables, simulates the copy on the case (gm.simulate plans the demand on that design) and reads the objective from gm.evaluate. The variables are searched with scipy.optimize.differential_evolution from a Latin hypercube with the given seed; the integers and the choices are searched as integers. One member is the plant as it is given (its own values, clipped to the bounds), so a start inside the bounds is among the trials and the best is never worse than it. It needs no gradient and its population gives the exact number of simulations. The number of doublets is a discrete variable: the search runs once for each count, from the lowest to the highest, on a new plant of that size, and the best trial of all counts wins; with gm.runtime.settings(processes=n) the searches of the counts run in n worker processes and give the same result. The plant and the case themselves are not changed.

A trial is feasible when its gm.evaluate.verdict has no reason a design can change (the reasons with a violation): no failed step, every requirement of the case passes, the supply temperatures of the services included, no operating limit breached. A reason no variable can change (a service the plant has no unit for) is not held against a trial; the verdict of the best plant, from gm.evaluate.report, keeps it. A trial that does not run (a physics error, say at some depth) is infeasible with the largest violation. The search sees feasible trials as better than infeasible ones, and infeasible ones ordered by their total relative violation, so it moves towards feasibility. The best feasible trial is best; with none, best is the least violating trial and feasible is False.

Parameters:

  • plant (Plant) – A plant with parameter values, and the ranges and options to search.

  • case (Case) – What the plant is asked to do: its demand is planned for each trial, so a variable that changes the design changes the plan, and its economics give the prices.

  • objective (str) – The metric to minimise, a KPI of gm.evaluate.totals or lcoh, lcoc, lcts, annualised_cost or co2; a metric to maximise is given with a leading "-".

  • evaluations (int) – The largest number of simulations for each doublet count; at least five per variable, and at least five. It has no default.

  • seed (int) – Seed of the search; the same call gives the same trials. It has no default.

  • plan (DataFrame, optional) – The run input held fixed over the trials, for a building block with no demand; without it each trial is planned from the case.

  • on_progress (callable, optional) – on_progress(done, total), called in the calling process after each finished trial, also when the searches run in worker processes; total is the number of trials the budget gives, and done reaches it at the end even when a search ends early. Raising gm.errors.Cancelled from it stops the search: no later trial starts, the workers are stopped and the exception propagates. The trials, their order and the result do not depend on it; any other exception it raises propagates too.

Returns:

{best, feasible, history, evaluations}. best is the objective as a Value in SI, whose parts["parameters"] are {name: Quantity} (an option is its string) and whose ["plant"] is the best plant: a Plant with the best doublet count and the values set, whose to_json is a plant file that runs. feasible is whether best is feasible; if not, best is the least violating trial that ran and its reason says why. history lists the same as best for every trial, with parts["violation"] (0 when feasible) and a reason for an infeasible trial: the first reason of its verdict (its dict), a text for a failed run. evaluations is the number of simulations. With no trial that ran, best has no value and no plant, and gives a reason. to_plain gives the JSON form.

Return type:

dict

Raises:

ValidationError – The issues of gm.check; invalid_design when the plant states nothing to search; missing_value for a range with one side only.
