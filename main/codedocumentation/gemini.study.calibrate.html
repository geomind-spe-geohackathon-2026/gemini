gemini.study.calibrate

gemini.study.calibrate(plant, dataset, params, start=None, end=None, backend='numpy', apply=True)[source]

Fit measured outputs with bounded least squares through the public replay API.

With apply=True, successful fits replace current parameters and persist as explicit overrides. No dated history entry is added. apply=False leaves the plant unchanged. Windows must lie in one design snapshot; holdout observations never enter the objective. The rows fitted are those where every target is measured and predicted at the initial values; equipment-specific starting guesses use the same rows. backend is the engine backend that evaluates the replays; the fit does not depend on it.
