gemini.physics.hydraulics

Pipe hydraulics: Darcy–Weisbach with Swamee–Jain, Beggs–Brill, path marching, radial inflow.

Inclinations are measured from horizontal [rad], positive when descending in surface-to-bottom order, as built by geometry.build_path().
