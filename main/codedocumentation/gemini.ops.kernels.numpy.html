gemini.ops.kernels.numpy

Array backend: NumPy ufuncs over whole time series, with floating-point warnings silenced.

Both branches of where are evaluated on every row, so the branch that is not selected may divide by zero or overflow; those warnings are silenced here and never reach the user. A None branch of where becomes NaN. Vector values (a parameter of shape (n,)) are indexed on their last axis, which leaves room for a leading scenario axis.
