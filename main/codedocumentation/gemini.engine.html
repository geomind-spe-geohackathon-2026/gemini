gemini.engine

One run of a plant on a case: Engine, simulate, Results, the schedule and the rest.

Clock and the executor registry are exported here; every other name is read from its file (gemini.engine.engine.Engine, gemini.engine.simulate.simulate, gemini.engine.results.Results, gemini.engine.schedule.schedule.schedule).

Modules

apply

The values of a case reach the modules of a plant by class, on a copy of the plant.

archive

Results as one .npz archive: typed arrays for numeric columns, JSON text for the rest.

bindings

The bindings of a run: which columns feed which module inputs, and in which order.

callbacks

Engine lifecycle callbacks: a JSON-lines event log and periodic checkpoints.

check

gm.check: what only the plant and the case together can say is missing or wrong.

checkpoint

Versioned checkpoint manifests with atomic publication of complete result generations.

chunkresult

ChunkResult: what an executor gives for one module over the rows of a chunk.

chunks

Chunks of ticks committed to Results, and their assembly into one table.

clock

Finite UTC clocks owned by the execution engine.

columns

The data one twin reads over a stretch of ticks, converted to SI, and what each tick lacks.

eager

The eager executor: forward on arrays over the whole chunk, row by row only when it must.

engine

One finite execution loop for assumed inputs, measured history and periodic live windows.

executor

Executors: the ways the engine runs a module over the rows of a chunk.

health

Health of a module over the rows of a chunk, as the columns of its result.

loader

Chunked time alignment of in-memory rows to finite ticks; it imports no engine code.

lockstep

Many runs side by side: answer the executor requests of several runs together.

results

Per-module series, energy integration, JSON views and lossless result persistence.

rowwise

Call a module for real, row by row, and stack the results of the rows into arrays.

runner

The one runner: chunks of ticks through the executor, then results and callbacks.

simulate

One run in one call: gm.simulate, gm.replay, gm.live and gm.resume.

twinresult

One twin evaluated over a block of ticks: its result columns, errors and new state.
