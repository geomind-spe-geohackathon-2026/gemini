gemini.units.display

The unit a quantity is shown in, and the conversion of a result to it.

DISPLAY_UNITS is the one table of quantity -> display unit: energy in MWh, power in MW, temperature in degC. It names no metric and no equipment: a folder that knows its own metrics (gm.evaluate) maps them to a quantity of this table. shown converts every Quantity of a result to the unit its SI unit has in a table. written_in and written give a stored file (a plant file) in the units its caller chose.

Module Attributes

DISPLAY_UNITS

For each quantity the unit it is shown in; a quantity not listed is shown in SI.
