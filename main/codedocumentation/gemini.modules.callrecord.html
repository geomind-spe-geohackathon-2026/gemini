gemini.modules.callrecord

The record of one module call in progress, and the helpers that read and shape its values.

The values of one call (resolved controls and boundaries, recorded outputs, the state it starts from and the results of child calls) live in a CallRecord held in the current_call context variable while the call runs; it is the only per-call state, so a module holds none. no_outputs() is the only other context setting.
