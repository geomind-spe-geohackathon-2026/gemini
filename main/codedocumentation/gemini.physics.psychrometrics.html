gemini.physics.psychrometrics

Moist-air psychrometrics: saturation pressure, dew point, humidity ratio and wet bulb.

All functions take SI inputs: absolute temperature [K], relative humidity as a fraction [-] and absolute pressure [Pa]. Moist air is treated as an ideal mixture of dry air and water vapour without the enhancement factor, which ASHRAE Fundamentals includes in its tabulated saturation humidity ratios (about 0.4 % at sea level); see humidity_ratio.

Module Attributes

MINIMUM_TEMPERATURE

Lower validity limit [K] of the Hyland-Wexler equation over ice (-100 degC).

MAXIMUM_TEMPERATURE

Upper validity limit [K] of the Hyland-Wexler equation over liquid water (200 degC).
