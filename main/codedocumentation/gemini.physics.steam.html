gemini.physics.steam

Saturated water and steam properties from IAPWS-IF97 and wet steam expansion.

Every function takes and returns SI values: pressure [Pa], temperature [K], specific enthalpy [J/kg] and specific entropy [J/kg/K]. The equations are the exact IAPWS-IF97 equations, not fits to them, so they reproduce the IF97 verification values to their nine printed digits:

  • region 4 saturation pressure (IF97 eq. 30) and saturation temperature (eq. 31);

  • region 1 compressed liquid basic equation (eq. 7, table 2) for enthalpy and entropy;

  • region 2 vapour basic equation (eqs. 15 to 17, tables 10 and 11) for enthalpy and entropy, with the B23 boundary equation (eq. 5) that limits region 2 above 623.15 K.

Reference: IAPWS (2007), Revised Release on the IAPWS Industrial Formulation 1997 for the Thermodynamic Properties of Water and Steam (IAPWS-R7-97(2012)); Wagner, W. et al. (2000), The IAPWS Industrial Formulation 1997, J. Eng. Gas Turbines Power 122, 150-182.

Saturated properties need regions 1 and 2, which border the saturation line only up to 623.15 K (16.529 MPa); region 3 is not implemented, so saturation states above 623.15 K raise PhysicsError. Geothermal flash plants operate far below this limit (separators at 0.2 to 2 MPa, condensers at 5 to 15 kPa). All properties are those of pure water; dissolved salts and non-condensable gases are not represented.

SATURATION_TOLERANCE

Relative pressure tolerance that keeps states computed on the saturation line in range.
