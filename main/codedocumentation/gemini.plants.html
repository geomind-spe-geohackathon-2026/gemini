gemini.plants

The plant classes and the functions of the plant dict.

Modules

atesplant

Low-temperature ATES plant: well pair, well pump, cooling heat exchanger and heat pump.

boilerchpplant

Nodal doublet with a heat exchanger, a boiler and a CHP on the secondary network.

coolingsubstation

Building cooling substation: valve, heat exchanger, the building's loads and its pump.

districtenergyplant

A district heating network and a district cooling network on an aquifer store.

districtnetwork

A two-pipe network feeding parallel substations.

doublet

A doublet: production and injection limbs, designed from its depth or set by hand.

doubletheatplant

The measured doublet and its surface equipment: filters, degasser, exchanger, pump.

heatingabsorptioncoolingplant

Heating with a peak boiler and an absorption chiller on the hot water.

heatingcoolingplant

Integrated heating and cooling on a geothermal doublet.

heatingfullcoolingplant

Heating with all three chillers: every unit but the heat pump.

heatingheatpumpcoolingplant

Heat pump heating with a peak boiler, and a compression chiller.

heatingplant

Geothermal heating: doublet, exchanger, peak boiler and the heating consumer.

heatingsubstation

Building heating substation: valve, heat exchanger, the building's demand and its pump.

multiwellplant

A well field: production and injection limbs joined by mixers and splitters.

nodaldoublet

Doublet produced against a wellhead pressure and injected through a solved pump head.

parallelexchangerplant

Nodal doublet with three parallel heat exchangers on one secondary network.

plant

Plants: a Module that connects its children and whose forward runs them in that order.

roles()

Return the closed table of roles as {role: {quantity: {"dimension", "over"}}}.
