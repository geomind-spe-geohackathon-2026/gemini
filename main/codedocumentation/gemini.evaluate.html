gemini.evaluate

What a finished run gave and whether it is acceptable.

Grouped by job: costs (capex, opex, annualised cost, levelised costs, NPV, payback), emissions (CO2), balances (energy, mass and plant balance, and the audits of one call), missing (the values of the economics the costs need), verdict (bounds, requirements, failures, the verdict) and summary and report (totals, capacities, the report and its comparison), coverage (what covered the demand of each day) and metrics (the metrics of a table with their display unit). Every function is gm.evaluate.<name>.

Modules

balances

The ledgers of a finished run and of one call: energy, mass, plant balance and the module audits.

costs

What a plant costs and earns: investment, operating cost, levelised costs, NPV and payback.

coverage

What covered the demand of each day: the geothermal plant, the backup, or nothing.

emissions

The CO2 a finished run emitted, and the CO2 a retained call tree emits.

metrics()

Return the metrics a table of results shows, each with its quantity and display unit.

missing(plant, case, results)

Return every value of the economics that the costs of a run need and the case lacks.

report(plant, case, results)

Return the energy, capacities, costs, CO2, requirements, verdict and failures of a run.

summary

What a finished run gave: the totals of its flows and the capacities of the plant.

verdict(plant, case, results)

Return whether a finished run is feasible, and why not.
