gemini.physics.fluid

Constant fluid properties inherited from GEMINI’s PVTConstantSTP model.

The defaults are the original constant-water values; pressure and temperature do not change them. gemini.streams.Fluid builds and caches one snapshot per fluid.

mixture_density, volume_flow, liquid_volume_flow and heat_capacity_rate carry no @ops.physics: Stream and FluidProperties call them on every eager step, the decorator would make those calls about 3.5 times slower, and they divide only by validated positive properties, so they need no eager scope.

Density [kg/m3] of the constant-water liquid of GEMINI's PVTConstantSTP model, near 4 degC.
