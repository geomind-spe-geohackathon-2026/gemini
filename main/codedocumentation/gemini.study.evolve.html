gemini.study.evolve

gemini.study.evolve(plant, case, objectives, evaluations, seed, *, population, polish, plan=None, on_progress=None)[source]

Return the best structures and designs of a superstructure, by a genetic algorithm.

The genome holds every choice the plant states: whether each optional module is on (set_optional), the group of each choice (set_choice), the number of doublets when size.doublets has a range, and the variables of gm.study.minimize (every range and options of a leaf, tied by role). A gene of a module that is off is ignored. With one objective it is a genetic algorithm; with two or more it is NSGA-II: the candidates are ranked in fronts of non-domination and, within a front, by crowding distance. A feasible candidate beats every infeasible one, and infeasible ones are ranked by their violation, as in minimize. Parents are chosen by binary tournament; continuous and integer genes cross by simulated binary crossover and mutate by polynomial mutation, the choices cross uniformly and mutate by a random reset; each generation keeps the best of the parents and children.

The first generation is the plant’s own values (clipped to the ranges) in each valid structure, in the order of plant.structures() while they fit, then random candidates. A candidate whose structure gives no valid plant (plant.with_structure(...).check() has an error) is refused before any simulation and counted in invalid. A candidate equal to one judged before in the genes that matter is not simulated again. The trials of a generation run in the worker processes of gm.runtime.settings(processes=n); the same seed gives the same result, whatever n. Each trial is judged as in minimize. The search ends when evaluations simulations have run, or after a number of generations in a row (STALL) without a new one.

With polish above 0, each of the first polish designs of the result (ordered by the first objective) is searched again by gm.study.minimize in the ranges its plant keeps, on the first objective, with population evaluations for each doublet count (the budget of minimize) and the same seed; its best plant is run once more for every objective and joins the designs. These simulations come on top of evaluations and count in the result.

Parameters:

  • plant (Plant) – A plant with values, with optional modules and choices and the ranges and options to search; a plant with neither is searched in its values alone.

  • case (Case) – What the plant is asked to do, as for minimize.

  • objectives (str or list of str) – The metric to minimise, or several (["lcts", "co2"]); a leading "-" maximises.

  • evaluations (int) – The largest number of simulations of the genetic search, at least population.

  • seed (int) – Seed of the search; the same call gives the same result.

  • population (int) – Candidates in each generation, at least 2.

  • polish (int) – The number of designs searched again by minimize; 0 for none. population must then be at least five per variable of the plant, the budget minimize needs.

  • plan (DataFrame, optional) – The run input held fixed over the trials, as for minimize.

  • on_progress (callable, optional) – on_progress(done, total) after each finished trial of the genetic search, total being evaluations; done reaches it when the search ends, before polish. Raising gm.errors.Cancelled stops the search.

Returns:

{front, best, history, evaluations, invalid}: front the designs no other beats on every objective (the best one alone for one objective), each a Design {structure, plant, metrics, feasible, reason, violation, parameters}; best the design best on the first objective (the least violating one when none is feasible); history per generation {generation, evaluations, best, front}: the simulations so far, the best value of each objective among the feasible candidates so far (None without one) and the size of the first front; evaluations the number of simulations; invalid the candidates refused for their structure.

Return type:

Evolution

Raises:

ValidationError – The issues of gm.check; invalid_design when no structure is valid or the plant states nothing to search; invalid_value for a setting out of its range.
