gemini.evaluate.verdict

gemini.evaluate.verdict(plant, case, results)[source]

Return whether a finished run is feasible, and why not.

The one place that decides feasibility; report, compare and the study use it. A run is feasible when it has no reason. A reason has a kind:

  • requirement: a requirement of the case, or a supply temperature of a service, that failed (requirements); no requirement could be checked; when some passed and none failed, each one that could not be judged (without a violation), except an idle unit and the supply of a service reported as not delivered; a service of the case that the run does not deliver.

  • failed_step: rows of the run that failed or were skipped.

  • numerical: a mass or energy residual above the tolerances of mass_balance, energy_balance and plant_balance, or a loop that did not converge.

  • bound: a recorded output outside the limits its module declares (bounds), or an output past an operating limit of a module (Module.operating_limits) in the steps in which the module carried flow.

The case is applied to the plant once; the plant of the user never changes.

Parameters:

  • plant (Module) – The plant of the run.

  • case (Case) – The case of the run.

  • results (Results) – A finished run.

Returns:

A dict {feasible, reasons} (to_dict() gives plain JSON). A reason is {kind, name, message} and, when it has them, path (the module or the output), value, limit (values) and worst_step (ISO 8601). A requirement reason of a check also has its role and quantity.

Return type:

Verdict
