gemini.physics.pumps

Pump relations: the TNO R11363 power polynomial, affinity-law curves, efficiencies.

The ESP and line-shaft pumps use the TNO R11363 power polynomial on the historical US basis (power, which the doublet design reads too, head and the ESP and line-shaft efficiencies and friction). The centrifugal pump uses a quadratic head curve in SI with a parabolic efficiency (centrifugal_head, parabolic_efficiency). The shared relations are the speed ratio, the hydraulic and shaft power and the operating window.
