gemini.physics.gas

A gas from its composition: molar mass, normal and ideal-gas density, heating value and CO2.

A composition maps a species to its mole fraction. The formulas of a fluid that carries a gas live here; gemini.streams.Fluid calls them once per fluid, so they run on plain numbers, except ideal_gas_density, which a stream calls with its pressure and temperature.

The end of the file holds the ideal-gas adiabatic compression of intercooled stages with an isentropic efficiency, the textbook form of GPSA Engineering Data Book, 14th ed. (2017), Section 13, with the compressibility factor Z = 1: T_2s = T_1 * r**((k - 1) / k) and T_2 = T_1 + (T_2s - T_1) / eta_s. It holds for gases near ideal behaviour (Z close to one, low reduced pressure) with a constant heat capacity ratio k = cp / cv over the stage.

Module Attributes

NORMAL_TEMPERATURE

0 degC, the normal state of DIN 1343.

NORMAL_PRESSURE

1.01325 bar(a), the normal state of DIN 1343.

SPECIES

Molar mass [kg/mol], lower heating value [J/mol] and carbon atoms burnt to CO2 per species.

COMPOSITION_TOLERANCE

Largest distance from one [-] of the sum of a composition; numerical, not physical.

AIR_COMPOSITION

Dry air as mole fractions, argon and CO2 counted as nitrogen (Incropera et al., Fundamentals of Heat and Mass Transfer, 7th ed., table A.4 lists air as one substance).

AIR_SPECIFIC_HEAT

Incropera et al., 7th ed., table A.4.
