gemini.study.size

gemini.study.size(plant, case)[source]

Return a new plant with first sizes from the case; the plant itself is not changed.

The sizes use gemini physics only. In order:

  • the number of doublets, the smallest in the range of size.doublets of the plant whose heat at the flow limit and the brine cooling limit of the case’s requirements covers the heating peak (the largest, with a note, when none does), for a plant class that takes the structural size doublets; the nominal flow of each doublet is the flow limit, the largest flow the case lets it run, so that its share in gm.evaluate.capacities is at most one;

  • the capacity of each boiler by the policy backup (below); the capacity of each heat_pump: the part of the heating peak the reservoir cannot reach, (supply - reservoir) / (supply - return) of it, shared by the units of the role, the reservoir temperature taken at the depth of the plant and the temperatures from demand.heating (a heat pump is left unsized, with a note, when the reservoir is hotter than the supply temperature); the capacity of each chiller: the cooling peak shared by the units, except the chiller that closes the chilled-water series, which gets the whole peak because a sorption unit delivers less than its rating when its characteristic map says so at the peak conditions and the last chiller makes up the difference;

  • the nominal_flow and nominal_head of each InjectionPump: the total nominal flow of the doublets its brine reaches, and the largest pressure rise one of them asks, the wellhead pressure that injects its flow less nominal_wellhead_pressure as the best suction, floored at zero (the injection well and aquifer of the case, gm.analysis’s solve_injection_pressure). A pump that serves no doublet of the plant, such as the one of a NodalDoublet, or whose doublet lacks a depth or a wellhead pressure, is left unsized with a note.

The heat pump share assumes an ideal exchanger: a finite approach leaves the water colder at the heat pump, which a boiler downstream makes up; a plant with a heat pump and no boiler may then fall short of its supply temperature at the peak.

A size that is not computed for a module kind (chp, heat_exchanger, a heat pump that is not needed) leaves it unchanged and lists it. The sized plant is then simulated once on the case and every requirement it breaks is listed: size never hides an infeasible start.

The plant states the design policy of the top-up units in params.backup, which has no default (a plant without it raises missing_value naming the path):

  • "none": a boiler covers only the heat the doublets cannot reach, the larger of the step of the heating plus the drive heat of the sorption chillers less the heat of the doublets at their limits and the part of it that the reservoir temperature cannot reach; a plant without doublets gets the peak;

  • "peak": a boiler covers the whole heating plus drive heat of the sorption chillers (the unit that draws heat from the boiler) at the step where they peak together, with the doublets off, so that it carries the peak when a doublet is out.

gm.check runs first and raises its issues, except the parameters of the plant that have no value: completing a template plant is what size is for.

Parameters:

  • plant (Plant) – A plant with parameter values and its backup.

  • case (Case) – The demand, the requirements on each doublet (a maximum production flow and brine cooling), the reservoir and the fluid.

Returns:

A new plant. Its attribute sizing holds the notes: {"sized": [...], "unchanged": [...], "broken": [...], "simulation": str}. Each size is {path, value, reason} with a Quantity value, each unit left unchanged {path, reason}, each broken requirement {unit_path, role, quantity, value, limit, worst_step} as gm.evaluate.requirements gives it, and simulation says simulated, or not simulated: <code>: <reason> when the sized plant cannot run (a value it still lacks, a design the case refuses).

Return type:

Plant

Raises:

ValidationError – missing_value naming params.backup or the path of what a size needs and the case or plant lacks; invalid_design for a plant that has doublets but whose class takes no structural size doublets.
