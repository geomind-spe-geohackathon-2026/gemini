gemini.evaluate.balances

The ledgers of a finished run and of one call: energy, mass, plant balance and the module audits.

energy_balance, mass_balance and plant_balance judge the columns of a finished run. call_energy_balance, call_mass_balance, collect_flows and call_emissions audit the retained evaluation tree of one gm.call.

A leaf exchanges energy outside its streams through its ports: power_in and power_out carry electricity and loss_out heat to the surroundings, signed. fuel is the one energy on no port of a leaf, and heat_accumulation the rate of increase of the energy a leaf stores, counted as an outflow. The legacy isothermal pumps do not allocate electricity to fluid enthalpy, mechanical work or environmental loss. Report that unresolved energy explicitly; never manufacture heat loss to force a first-law residual to zero.

Largest relative residual of a module, in or out, the larger of the two; numerical.

PLANT_TOLERANCE

Largest relative residual of the root; numerical, the iterations of a loop stop near it.

ENERGY_INPUTS

Energy [W] that enters a leaf from outside its streams, as collect_flows names it.

ENERGY_OUTPUTS

Energy [W] that leaves a leaf other than through its streams.

EDGE_CATEGORIES

The categories of the modules at the plant's edge.
