gemini.physics.ground

Ground and aquifer heat transport: doublet breakthrough, conduction shapes, Ramey profiles.

The functions are plain SI relations used by the subsurface equipment: the thermal breakthrough of a geothermal doublet (Gringarten and Sauty 1975), the conduction conductance of buried storage volumes (Incropera et al.; Carslaw and Jaeger 1959), the steady-flux borehole cell resistance (Hellstrom 1991) and the transient rock resistance and fluid temperature profile of a well in a linear geothermal gradient (Ramey 1962).
