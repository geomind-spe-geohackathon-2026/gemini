gemini.evaluate.report

gemini.evaluate.report(plant, case, results)[source]

Return the energy, capacities, costs, CO2, requirements, verdict and failures of a run.

The costs are read with on_error="record": a cost that the case or the plant cannot give is a value None with a reason, so that reports of many designs stay comparable.

Parameters:

  • plant (Module) – The plant of the run.

  • case (Case) – The case of the run.

  • results (Results) – A finished run.

Returns:

A dict with heat_delivered, cold_delivered, boiler_heat, electricity_bought and gas_bought (J over the run, Quantity values; boiler_heat is the heat of the boilers), capacities (see capacities), capex, opex, lcts, lcoh, lcoc and co2 (values; capex and opex keep their parts, per module and per kind), requirements (the list of requirements), passed (False if any requirement failed, True if none failed and one passed, else None), verdict (gm.evaluate.verdict, the one answer compare reads), failed_steps (the number of steps that failed), failures (the failed and skipped rows grouped by module path and code, see failures; empty when nothing failed) and services (the services the case asks for, "heating" and "cooling"). to_dict(units=None) gives plain JSON in display units.

Return type:

Report
