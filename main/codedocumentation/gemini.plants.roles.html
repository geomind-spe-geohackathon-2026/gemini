gemini.plants.roles

gemini.plants.roles()[source]

Return the closed table of roles as {role: {quantity: {"dimension", "over"}}}.

A requirement is on a role and a quantity of it. The roles are doublet, heat_pump, boiler, chp, chiller (absorption, adsorption and compression), heat_exchanger, heating_supply, cooling_supply and plant. dimension is the quantity of the bounds, None for a number without one; over says whether the quantity is read per step and takes over, or is a window quantity or a property that takes none.

heat_output of heat_pump, boiler and chp is the heat the unit gives its water and cold_output of chiller the cold it takes from its chilled water, in W, from the enthalpy flows of its ports (the source side for a heat pump in cooling mode). reinjection_ratio of doublet is the injected over the produced liquid volume flow. count of doublet is the number of doublets of the plant and unmet_share of a supply the unmet energy over the energy demanded by the service, both over the whole period. The role plant is the plant itself (unit_path ""): electricity_bought, gas_bought and heat_surplus are powers in W as gm.evaluate.totals sums them; co2 is the mass in kg of CO2-equivalent emitted in each step, as gm.evaluate.co2 sums it, so over: sum of it is the mass of the run (over: each judges the mass of a step, which depends on its length). sum of a power adds the step values, as for every other power. gas_burned of plant is the co-produced gas burned in the plant, in kg/s: what its gas makeups give to a burner and what its flares burn, from the mass flows of their ports.
