gemini.engine.chunks

Chunks of ticks committed to Results, and their assembly into one table.

A chunk holds the ticks of one evaluation as columns. Whatever its length, one tick or a whole window, it goes through the same code. Columns are NumPy arrays or, for objects such as list outputs, Python lists; the table built from several chunks is lazy.
