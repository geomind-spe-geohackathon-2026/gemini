gemini.ops.registry

The op registry: the name, kind and operand count of every op.

The registry only describes ops; backends implement them (gemini.ops.kernels) and the graph records them. The kind is what graph passes rely on: elementwise ops apply row by row, index selects one item of a vector parameter, check produces per-row error codes, solver ops (brentq, newton) find roots with per-row stopping and keep their function and settings in node attributes (the arity counts the fixed operands, args follow), and scan carries state from step to step. A break node runs one module that cannot be traced, row by row on the plain path; its outputs are break_output nodes. A loop node is the masked fixed point of a plant loop: its body runs inside the interpreter, pass by pass.
