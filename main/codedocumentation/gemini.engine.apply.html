gemini.engine.apply

The values of a case reach the modules of a plant by class, on a copy of the plant.

apply_case sets, for a run, what the plant dict does not hold: the ground on every Formation, the reservoir and the fluid on every reservoir and Doublet (at the depth of the doublet), the air on every Atmosphere, the grid gas on every GasGrid, the supply temperature plus the margin on every unit of a supply line, and, for the consumers (HeatingGrid, CoolingGrid), the desander (site.fluid) and the grid connection (utilities.electricity), every parameter and boundary that declares a case path (case=), and checks the states a stateful module starts from (initial_state); started hands them to the run. One private function does each kind. A value the plant needs and the case lacks raises missing_value naming its path in the case.
