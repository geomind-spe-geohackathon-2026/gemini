gemini

GEMINI: a general geothermal heating and cooling package.

Modules

analysis

Engineering questions about one piece of equipment at fixed conditions: wells, pumps, fits.

case

The case: the outside conditions over one period and the rules of third parties.

compiler

The compiler: trace a plant into a computation graph and register it as an executor.

engine

One run of a plant on a case: Engine, simulate, Results, the schedule and the rest.

errors

The seven error classes, GeminiWarning and the fixed code lists, as gm.errors.<Name>.

evaluate

What a finished run gave and whether it is acceptable.

modules

Public composable equipment and module API.

ops

Ops that physics is written with, so one function runs on floats, arrays and traced values.

physics

GEMINI physics: the textbook formulas, one topic per file.

plants

The plant classes and the functions of the plant dict.

runtime

Context-local numerical and display settings: gm.runtime.settings and gm.runtime.state.

streams

What flows between modules: Stream, Fluid and the ports that carry them.

study

Search and sample plant designs through the public run functions.

units

Unit conversions at the package boundaries: gm.units.
