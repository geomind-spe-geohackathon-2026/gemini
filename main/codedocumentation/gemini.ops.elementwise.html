gemini.ops.elementwise

Elementwise functions and operators of the physics: exp, log, sqrt, maximum, …

Write ops.maximum(a, b) instead of max(a, b), ops.sqrt instead of math.sqrt and ops.logical_or instead of or, so one source runs on floats, arrays and traced values. None of them raises on an input outside its domain: log(0) is -inf, log(-1) and sqrt(-1) are nan and exp(1000) is inf, as in NumPy, because both branches of where are always evaluated.
