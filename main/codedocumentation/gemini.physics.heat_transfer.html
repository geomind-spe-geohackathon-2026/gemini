gemini.physics.heat_transfer

Pipe heat loss, heat-exchanger effectiveness relations and lumped thermal stores.

Module Attributes

DEFAULT_CP

Liquid heat capacity [J/kg/K] assumed when measurements give none; the Fluid default.

DUTY_AGREEMENT_TOLERANCE

Largest relative difference [-] of the hot and cold duties for which an apparent UA is taken.

DETERMINED

determined, or the first reason that it is not.

NONPOSITIVE_INPUT

determined, or the first reason that it is not.

DISAGREEING_DUTIES

determined, or the first reason that it is not.

NO_LMTD

determined, or the first reason that it is not.

DEFAULT_ICE_LATENT_HEAT

the conventional 333.55 kJ/kg at 0 degC and 101.325 kPa.

DEFAULT_FREEZING_TEMPERATURE

Normal melting point of ice [K].

DEFAULT_WATER_HEAT_CAPACITY

Specific heat capacity [J/kg/K] of saturated liquid water at 0.01 degC (IAPWS-95, Wagner and Pruss 2002).

DEFAULT_ICE_HEAT_CAPACITY

Specific heat capacity [J/kg/K] of ice Ih at the triple point (IAPWS R10-06).

MAX_SEGMENTS

liquid, freezing or melting, then solid (or back).
