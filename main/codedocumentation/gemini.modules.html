gemini.modules

Public composable equipment and module API.

Modules

bindings

Portable sensor and dated-parameter metadata of a module: which column feeds which input.

call

One call of one module from plain JSON.

callargs

How a call is read: its inlets and its controls and boundaries, and the ports it gives back.

callrecord

The record of one module call in progress, and the helpers that read and shape its values.

check(cls[, example])

Return a report of gemini's module rules for a new Module class; it never raises.

classpath

The path that plant files and checkpoints store for a module class, and the import back.

container

Ordered list and mapping containers for child modules.

control

GEMINI control modules.

cooling

GEMINI thermally driven cooling modules.

declarations

Declarations registered by gemini.modules.Module.

display

How a module shows itself: parameter values in display units, and its declarations.

environment

GEMINI edge modules of the surroundings: the air and the rock around the wells.

exchangers

GEMINI heat exchange modules.

grids

GEMINI edge modules: what the plant buys from outside and delivers to outside.

heating

GEMINI heat generation modules.

module

Equipment modules: declarations and children registered in __init__, called with inlets.

piping

GEMINI piping equipment modules.

power

GEMINI power generation and grid equipment modules.

pumps

GEMINI pumps equipment modules.

results

Health and immutable per-call module results.

states

The state of a module tree: what a call starts from and how a step advances it.

storage

GEMINI thermal energy storage modules.

subsurface

GEMINI subsurface equipment modules.

treatment

GEMINI treatment equipment modules.

tree

The module tree: children, paths and the listings of declarations below a module.

values

The values of a module tree: parameters, controls and boundaries, their validity and snapshots.

wells

GEMINI well modules.
